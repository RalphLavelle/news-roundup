US–Iran war

Talks are still stuck. Reuters reports Iran told Pakistan's army chief it won't talk to Washington until its demands are met; senior Iranian officials see no deal before the US midterms. Iran wants Hormuz reopened first; the US wants nuclear talks first. Foreign Minister Araqchi blamed the US for the Hormuz tensions.

Hegseth made an unannounced visit to Israel to discuss Iran with Netanyahu. Iraq closed the Sheeb crossing with Iran until further notice. AFP covers Tehran shopkeepers hit by rising prices and two men in the UK re-arrested on suspicion of spying for Iran.
